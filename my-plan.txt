The Asynchronous Bureaucracy Buster (Track: Taskmaster)
The Problem: Disputing insurance claims, medical bills, or canceling complex subscriptions is a messy, multi-step, weeks-long chore.
The Agent: A background worker that takes a scanned bill, figures out why it was denied, and fights it for you.

How it works: It uses Gemini 3.5 to parse the uploaded bill, searches the web or uploaded policy documents for coverage rules, drafts an appeal, and sends it via an email API.

The "Agentic" magic: It checks an inbox daily for a response. If the insurance company asks for more info, the agent pings you for it, then automatically replies to them.

Why judges will love it: It solves a highly relatable, frustrating real-world problem, proves it can maintain context over days/weeks (asynchronous operations), and takes actual action (sending emails/drafting forms).
